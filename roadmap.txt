NOTES FOR YOUR TRANSITION
================================================================================

1. Your Node.js/Full-stack background is a STRENGTH:
   - MCP servers can be built in TypeScript/Node.js
   - API design, system architecture skills transfer directly
   - Frontend skills useful for Streamlit/demo apps

2. Focus 70-80% on SOFTWARE ENGINEERING aspects:
   - Building production systems, APIs, pipelines
   - System design, error handling, deployment
   - The "engineering" in AI Engineering matters most

3. Focus 20-25% on AI/ML THEORY:
   - Enough to understand what's happening under the hood
   - Don't get stuck on math - understand concepts, move to building

4. Items EXCLUDED (not needed for Agentic AI Engineer role):
   - Deep reinforcement learning
   - Traditional NLP (dependency parsing, POS tagging)
   - Research-level GAN training (basic understanding sufficient)
   - Hardware/chip-level optimization

5. Priority Order if time is limited:
   Phase 5 (LangChain/LangGraph) > Phase 6 (RAG) > Phase 7 (MCP) >
   Phase 8 (Production) > Phase 3 (Transformers) > Phase 4 (Fine-tuning) >
   Phase 2 (Deep Learning) > Phase 1 (Foundations)

   (Given your experience, you can learn foundations in parallel while building)
